Your hand

    How the deck works

    The deck holds 27 cards in three kinds: warm-ups, main games and cool-downs. A five-minute hand has a warm-up and a main game, ten minutes adds a cool-down, fifteen minutes adds a second main game.

    Only cards that fit your spot, your beagle’s nose level and the things you ticked are dealt. Main games match your level when there are enough of them. The daily deal stays the same all day for the same choices, so everyone at home plays the same cards. Press New deal for a different hand.

    Your played days are saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent to us, and there is no account.

    Keep it a game

    • Short and happy wins: stop while your beagle still wants more.
    • Make the first round easy, so every game starts with a find.
    • Use the same cue words every time, like “Find it!” and “All done”.
