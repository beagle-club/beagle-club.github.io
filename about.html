About Beagle Guide

Beagle Club is edited by Tom Rowley, who started collecting beagle questions after noticing how many of them begin with "is it normal that…". Howling at the neighbour's cat, escaping through a gap you didn't know existed, gaining weight on the same food: the answers are usually more interesting than they look.

Tom is a writer, not a vet. The articles pull together veterinary references, breed studies and owner experience, and the sources sit right next to the claims they support. When the numbers are uncertain, you will see a range rather than a single confident figure.

Corrections are welcome. Beagles change, research changes, and a guide that is wrong helps nobody.
